About me

I'm a UI/UX and Product Designer who enjoys turning ideas into meaningful digital experiences. My approach combines creative thinking, user-centred design, and a strong attention to detail to create interfaces that feel intuitive, visually cohesive, and purposeful.

I work across the product design process, from understanding user needs and exploring ideas to mapping user journeys, creating wireframes, developing interactive prototypes, and refining high-fidelity interfaces. I care about how a product looks, how it works for its users, and how effectively it solves real problems.

My experience spans projects across manufacturing, education, fintech, e-commerce, and other digital product environments. These projects have strengthened my ability to approach different problems, organise complex information, and translate business requirements into clear and accessible user experiences.

Beyond interface design, I enjoy exploring visual identity, design systems, and the small details that make digital products feel polished and consistent. My goal is to create thoughtful design solutions that serve users, support business objectives, and make technology easier to understand and use.
